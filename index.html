AN EVERETT GAME

TOILET GOALIE

  • Drag the ball to dodge. Flick it, or tap where you want it to go.
  • Bounce off cars for points. Every 5 points wins back a lost roll!
  • Poop costs a toilet paper roll. So does getting caught!
  • Daytime: giant feet, football cannons, Venus fly traps, and paint drops with huskies inside. A husky bite turns you into someone else!
  • Dark: hide under beds from the dolls. If a spider web gets you, you start over at zero!

TOILETGOALIE

EPISODE I

THE FLUSH
AWAKENS

Everett says: “Let’s make a game about a toilet bad guy that shoots water and poop! There’s a soccer ball you control with your finger, and you aim it where you want it to go. Try to get it in the goal without getting caught by the goalie. The goalie is a toilet. Also, it’s kind of like Resident Evil sometimes. And the music should be rock music.”

Everett says: “Can you add spiders? If they web-shoot you when it’s dark, you have to start over again with zero points. And there are baby dolls, and you gotta hide under beds. And there are giants, and during the daytime you don’t want to get hit by their feet.”

“And there’s smoke coming out of a Resident Evil car, because there are cars all around, and you want to bounce the ball off of them. That gets you another point. And if you bounce one in, you get an extra point. And there are football cannons.”

Everett says: “I want Venus flytraps that can eat the ball. And paint drops that you don’t want to get hit by. They have huskies in them, and you don’t want to get bitten. If you get bitten, you switch into another character, like a man or a football man. Also, I’m thinking we add a way to earn points to get our lost lives back.”

And so the game was made. Now it is up to YOU to get the ball past the toilet....
