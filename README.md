# Toilet Goalie

A soccer game invented by Everett. Get the ball past a toilet goalie that shoots water and poop, with rock music, giants, Venus fly traps, paint drops with huskies inside, and spooky lights-out levels full of creepy dolls, spiders and toilet-paper zombies.

Built for phones. Open `index.html` in a browser, or turn on GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch → `main` / root) to play it at a link.

## How to play

- **Drag** the ball to dodge. **Flick** it, or **tap** where you want it to go.
- Get it past the toilet into the goal. If the toilet catches it, you lose a toilet paper roll.
- **Water** knocks you around. **Poop** costs a roll.
- **Cars** on the sides: every bounce is +1 point. Bounce one in for a bank shot bonus.
- Every **5 points** wins back a lost roll (up to 5).

## Levels

| Level | What shows up |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Just the toilet and the cars |
| 2 | Giant feet + Venus fly traps |
| 3 | Lights out: creepy dolls. Hide under the beds! |
| 4 | Football cannons + paint drop huskies |
| 5 | Giants + fly traps + paint huskies |
| 6 | Lights out: spiders. A web sends you back to zero points! |
| 7+ | Daytime: everything. Every 3rd level: lights out with dolls, spiders and zombies |

A husky bite turns you into **Man** (slower) or **Football Man** (wobbly shots) for 10 seconds.

Everything (graphics, music, sound effects) is drawn and synthesized in code. No image or audio files.
