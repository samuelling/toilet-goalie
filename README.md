# Toilet Goalie

A soccer game invented by Everett. Get the ball past a toilet goalie that shoots water and poop, with rock music, giants, Venus fly traps, paint drops with huskies inside, and spooky lights-out levels full of creepy dolls, spiders and toilet-paper zombies.

Built for phones. Open `index.html` in a browser, or turn on GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch → `main` / root) to play it at a link.

## How to play

- **Drag** the ball to dodge. **Flick** it, or **tap** where you want it to go.
- Get it past the toilet into the goal. If the toilet catches it, you lose a toilet paper roll.
- **Water** knocks you around. **Poop** costs a roll.
- **Cars** on the sides: every bounce is +1 point. Bounce one in for a bank shot bonus.
- Every **5 points** wins back a lost roll (up to 5).

## Levels

| Level | What shows up |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Just the toilet and the cars |
| 2 | Giant feet + Venus fly traps |
| 3 | Lights out: creepy dolls. Hide under the beds! |
| 4 | Football cannons + paint drop huskies |
| 5 | Giants + fly traps + paint huskies |
| 6 | Lights out: spiders. A web sends you back to zero points! |
| 7 | Kayaks on the river + synthesizers (synth music!) |
| 8 | Kayaks + synthesizers + cannons + paint huskies |
| 9 | Lights out: dolls, spiders and zombies |
| 10–12 | Kayaks plus a mix of everything; level 12 is lights out |
| **13** | **FINAL BOSS: the Fire Rock Guy.** He shoots fireballs that leave fire on the ground. Hit him with the ball (water balls do double damage, fire balls do nothing) or score past him. Beat him to reach the **true ending**: grab the golden gem and escape before the bad guys catch you, then roll the credits. |
| 14+ | Keep playing: the mix continues, every 3rd level lights out |

Grab a glowing orb to become a **Fire** ball (burns poop and fly traps, faster shots), a **Water** ball (drinks water shots, washes off paint, swims the river), or a **Fire & Water** ball (all of that, plus a bonus point per goal). Sometimes a level starts you with one.

Kayaks poke you (lose a roll), but kick the ball into one to flip it for +1. Synthesizers send out WUB rings that push you, and bouncing a shot off one is +1.

Huskies bring fire: flames burst up where they land and trail behind them. Touching fire costs a roll (water balls put it out). Poop that misses you lands as a poop puddle; step in one and you get stuck, then turn into a **tiny pee drop** for 10 seconds.

From level 2, **cages** sometimes drop from the sky (watch for the flashing shadow). If one traps you, tap like crazy to break out, and you become a **FART MACHINE** for 10 seconds: fart-powered kicks fly faster, stinky gas clouds scare off huskies, dolls and zombies, and a fart-shot goal is worth +1.

A husky bite turns you into **Man** (slower) or **Football Man** (wobbly shots) for 10 seconds. Whenever you're a guy, the field turns into a **jungle** with vines, bongo drums and poisonous mushrooms: touching one costs a roll and makes you woozy, but kicking the ball into one squishes it for +1.

Everything (graphics, music, sound effects) is drawn and synthesized in code. No image or audio files.
